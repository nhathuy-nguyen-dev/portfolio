About me

I'm 21 years old, originally from Trondheim, and moved to Oslo to study Applied Computer Technology at OsloMet. I got my first taste of programming through the IT1 course in my final year of videregående. Since then it's been a rollercoaster, but the real turning point came when I started university and decided to take programming seriously.

Through my studies I've developed a strong interest in programming, web development and digital solutions. Alongside my studies I work at Renow AS, where I contribute with SEO, client contact, marketing and adapting digital solutions. I'm motivated by learning new technologies, solving problems and putting what I know to work in practical projects.
